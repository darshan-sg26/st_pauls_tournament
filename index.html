OFFICIAL TOURNAMENT GUIDELINES

🏏 SHORT PITCH CRICKET TOURNAMENT

Rules and Regulations

Please read all tournament rules carefully. All participating teams must strictly adhere to the guidelines set by the Sports Club of ST PAULS COLLEGE.

01

Team Composition

Squad Guidelines
  • ✓ Each team must consist of 8 players.
  • ✓ Only registered players are allowed to participate.
02

Match Format

Tournament Structure
  • ✓ Each match will be played for 5 overs per team.
  • ✓ The tournament will follow a knockout format.
  • ✓ The team losing a match will be eliminated from the tournament.
03

Batting Powerplay

Double Runs Feature
  • ✓ Each team will be allowed one Batting Powerplay over per innings.
  • ✓ During the Powerplay over, all runs scored will be doubled.
  • ✓ The Powerplay over must be declared to the umpire before the over begins.
  • ✓ The umpire's decision regarding Powerplay runs will be final.
04

Standing Throw Rule

Bowling Regulations
  • ✓ The ball must be delivered using a standing throw action.
  • ✓ Running or taking a full run-up before delivering the ball is not permitted.
  • ✓ The umpire will decide whether the delivery follows the rules.
05

Umpire's Decision

Authority & Discipline
  • ✓ The umpire's decision on all matters, including dismissals, runs, wides, no-balls and rule violations, shall be FINAL AND BINDING.
  • ✓ Arguments or misconduct towards the umpire will not be tolerated.
06

Registration

Deadlines & Policy
  • ✓ The last date for team registration is November 10th.
  • ✓ No on-the-spot registrations will be accepted.
  • ✓ Teams must complete registration before the deadline to participate.
07

General Rules

Code of Conduct
  • ✓ All teams must report on time for their scheduled matches.
  • ✓ Teams must maintain discipline and sportsmanship throughout the tournament.
  • ✓ Any disputes or misconduct will be handled by the tournament organisers.
  • ✓ The organisers reserve the right to make decisions regarding unforeseen circumstances.

PLAY FAIR. PLAY HARD. RESPECT THE GAME! 🏏

Best of luck to all participating teams!
